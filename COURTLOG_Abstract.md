# COURTLOG

*Project Abstract — COUCH 2026*

Nigeria's courts continue to lose time and case integrity to problems that sit outside the reach of ongoing e-filing efforts: physical case files go missing between registry and courtroom, adjournments compound with no early warning, and judgments won without enforcement quietly lapse into non-compliance. COURTLOG is a case-tracking platform built to close that gap, following a matter from the moment it is filed to the moment a judgment is actually enforced.

The platform has four integrated modules. A QR-based chain-of-custody system tracks every physical file through filing, registry, and courtroom checkpoints, flagging any file unscanned for seven or more days. A Hearing Compliance Engine lets clerks log call-over outcomes in a single tap and pushes the next hearing date directly to parties and counsel through WhatsApp, the channel Nigerian registries already use for hearing notices. A predictive Delay-Risk Model, trained on case type, court, adjournment history, and time elapsed, flags matters statistically likely to stall before they do — the project's core AI component. An Execution Tracker closes the loop after judgment: it generates enforcement instruments such as the Writ of Fi Fa and Garnishee proceedings under the Judgment Enforcement Rules, logs sheriff action against them, and flags any matter with no enforcement action after ninety days as non-compliant.

COURTLOG is deliberately positioned as a complement to, not a competitor of, the electronic filing and virtual court administration systems the Federal High Court is already deploying. E-filing addresses document submission; COURTLOG addresses everything after — physical custody, adjournment compliance, delay prediction, and post-judgment enforcement — that e-filing does not reach.

The project's feasibility rests on an active institutional pathway rather than an assumption of goodwill. The concept was reviewed directly by the Deputy Chief Registrar of the Federal High Court, Barrister Antonia Oyibo, whose feedback shaped the current design — including the shift from SMS to WhatsApp for hearing notices, reflecting what registries already use. A formal proposal for implementation has been submitted to the Chief Registrar's office, subject to the Chief Judge's approval, giving COURTLOG a named, documented route from prototype to institutional adoption.

COURTLOG addresses the Public Sector / e-Governance track, with its predictive delay-risk layer additionally qualifying it for the Best AI Innovation Award.
